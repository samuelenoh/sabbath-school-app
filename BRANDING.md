# Official Seventh-day Adventist church identity

The application uses the unmodified **English Standard Logo** from the Seventh-day Adventist World Church's identity system. These are the official symbol-and-wordmark assets, rather than recreated flame artwork or a regional division logo.

- Official download page: https://www.adventist.design/downloads/logo/
- Source SVG archive: https://cdn.adventist.org/adventist-logo/2/en/adventist-en-svg--standard.zip
- Usage guidance: https://www.adventist.design/global-elements/the-church-symbol/
- Retrieved: 14 October 2026.

SVG artwork, colors, path data and proportions are unchanged from the official archive. Source control normalizes line endings. The hashes below identify the files packaged with this application:

The white version appears on the green navigation and sign-in backgrounds; the forest version appears on the light startup card. The original proportions and symbol/wordmark arrangement are preserved. Logo images have no filter, animation, stretching, or decorative enclosing box, and sit apart from the application's Sabbath School department label. Loading animation applies only to a separate spinner.

The official TM variants are used because the app does not establish trademark registration status in Ghana; the official download page directs users to choose the registration designation applicable to their country. The church retains its trademark rights. These files are church identity assets, not a grant of a general software or trademark license.
